# Storylines

All copy and numbers come from the app source (`ai-accountant-source.tar.gz`):
the invoice ABC Office Supplies / INV-2025-001 / $234.50 / Office Expenses (InvoiceUpload.jsx),
the dashboard rows (Dashboard.jsx), "reduce manual data entry by 90%" and "START MY FREE DEMO" (Home.jsx).

## A. One invoice, start to books (recommended, built)

Device: one object across the product. Form: object-to-object chain.
A real paper invoice is buried under a growing pile; it is carried into Upload Invoice, the AI fills
every field, Save Invoice irises open the Dashboard where the same $234.50 lands in Recent Transactions.

- First 2 s: the invoice fills the left of a desk, "Another invoice to type in" lands beside it.
- Beats: hook (invoice) → tension (pile grows) → question → carry into Upload → extraction → save → lands in Dashboard → 90% claim → end card.
- Look: warm desk laminate, then the product's own gray-50/white UI with blue-600; brand blue→purple gradient only on the claim, the mark and the CTA.
- Sound: a light pulse bed that opens when the product appears; click on Save; pop on the 95% badge. (Delivered silent: no music source in this session.)

Why: a stranger follows one piece of paper they recognise all the way into the books.
